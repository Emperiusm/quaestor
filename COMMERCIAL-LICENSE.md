# Commercial licensing

Quaestor is released under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md). That licence
covers non-commercial use only. Any commercial use needs a separate commercial licence.

## Free under the licence

No licence purchase is needed for:

- **Research, experiments and testing** for the benefit of public knowledge
- **Personal study**, hobby projects and private entertainment
- **Charities, educational institutions, public research organisations, public safety or health
  organisations, environmental protection organisations and government institutions**, whatever
  their funding

Anyone who shares the software must pass on the licence terms and the `Required Notice:` line at
the top of [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md). If you use Quaestor in published research, please cite it;
[`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) has the details, and GitHub's **Cite this repository** button
formats them for you.

## Needs a commercial licence

Commercial use includes, for example:

- Using Quaestor in the work of a company or other for-profit organisation, including purely
  internal engineering use
- Offering Quaestor, or a service built on it, to paying customers
- Bundling or embedding Quaestor in a commercial product

If you're unsure whether your use counts as commercial, ask before you rely on it.

## How to get one

Email **admin@slabsofficial.com** with:

- your organisation's name
- how you intend to use Quaestor
- roughly how many people or machines will use it

## Contributions

By submitting a contribution (a pull request, patch or other change), you agree that the project
maintainer may distribute your contribution under both the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0
and the project's commercial licences. This is what lets commercial licences cover the whole
codebase.
